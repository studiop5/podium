PODIUM is a free, open-source reader for sheet music. It runs in your web browser and opens any score stored as a PDF file, from your own device or from a cloud storage service.

Choose how the pages of your score are laid out: as a book, as a table, or as a horizontal or vertical scroll. Turn the pages with a touch, a key, or a foot pedal while you play.

You can write on your scores with a pen or a pencil, add text and music symbols, draw new staves, and save your annotations with the score. A metronome, a piano, a tuner, a stopwatch and a clock are there to help you practice.
